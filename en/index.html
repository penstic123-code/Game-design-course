Interactive course

The Hand-Drawn Game Design Notebook

29 interactive web chapters that teach game design from scratch. No programming needed.

🎮 What it covers

Why games are fun, and how to turn a fun idea into a real game. From the core loop and player motivation through balance and ethics, ending with a prototype of your own.

👥 Who it is for

  • Beginners who want to make games
  • Students of design, product or programming who want to understand games
  • Players of many games who want to know “why is it fun?”

No background needed.

✅ What you will be able to do

  • Take apart a game’s core loop
  • Design and playtest a paper prototype
  • See why a game keeps people hooked

Time: 29 chapters of about 30 minutes each, roughly 15 hours in all. Tips: click a section title to fold it; the ☰ at the bottom right is the chapter outline; progress and notes are saved in your browser.

🗺️ The learning map: walk all 29 chapters like a board game

Start at square 1 and follow the winding route; click any square to jump there. Colors show the part each chapter belongs to.

Every chapter has: learning goals, diagrams, examples, an invention story, sources for the principles, interactive experiments, common misconceptions, reflections and controversies, and a one-line summary. What you finish with is a complete toolkit running from concepts and principles to a finished piece of work.
